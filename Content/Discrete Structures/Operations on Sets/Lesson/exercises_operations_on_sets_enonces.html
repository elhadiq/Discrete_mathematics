4Operator precedence medium

  1. $A \cup B \cap C$  (meaning $A \cup (B \cap C)$)
  2. $(A \cup B) \cap C$
  3. $A' \cap B \cup C$  (meaning $(A' \cap B) \cup C$)

  • 15 use all three.
